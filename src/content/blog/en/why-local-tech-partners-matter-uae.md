Choosing software is not only about features. It is about what happens after you pay, when a report looks wrong on a busy evening, a new staff member needs training, or you want to add a branch. For small and medium businesses in the UAE, the partner behind the software often matters as much as the software itself.

## The problem with "cheap now, expensive later"

Many UAE business owners have a similar story: software that looked affordable, then extra charges for setup, training and every small change, and support that was hard to reach or did not understand their business. That experience is exactly why MarkZone started. We saw businesses paying excessive prices for software and getting poor support afterwards.

## What a local partner understands

A partner based in the UAE understands the details that global, one-size-fits-all systems often miss:

- **Arabic and English**, for staff, customers and receipts
- **VAT-ready reports** that make your accountant’s job easier
- **AED pricing** without currency surprises
- **How local shops actually work**: consignment stores, salons that live on WhatsApp, tailors who save shaila and ghutra measurements

Our apps are made for specific industries: [OxPOS](/oxpos) for retail, [ZainaApp](/zainaapp) for salons and spas, and [TexPOS](/texpos) for tailoring shops.

## Support you can actually reach

When something goes wrong, you want a real person who answers, in your time zone, in your language. At MarkZone, every client gets full WhatsApp support daily from 10 AM to 10 PM (Sunday off), plus on-site visits when the situation requires. Outstanding service is the standard for every customer, never an upgrade.

## Speed matters

Small businesses cannot afford long projects. A local partner that knows its products can move quickly. Our apps are typically running the same day, with setup taking between 45 minutes and 2 hours. Websites are delivered within 2 weeks, and custom apps are fully delivered from about 2 months depending on size.

## Honest, clear pricing

A good partner tells you the price up front. Our apps have a one-time setup price that includes the first year, then a low yearly renewal, with no hidden costs. Websites and custom projects get a clear quote before work starts.

## Questions to ask any software provider

Whoever you choose, ask:

1. Who do I contact when there is a problem, and when are they available?
2. Is training included, and can you move my existing data?
3. What does it cost after the first year?
4. Does the system work in Arabic and English, with VAT-ready reports?
5. Can you visit if something needs to be fixed on site?
