Beauty is a busy, competitive industry in the UAE. Clients expect to book easily, be served on time, and be recognised when they return. Behind the scenes, owners juggle appointments, staff, commissions, products and cash. The salons that grow are rarely the ones with the flashiest interiors. They are the ones that run in an organized way.

Here are the practical shifts we see helping salons, barbershops and spas in 2026.

## 1. From the paper book to one shared calendar

The appointment book at reception works until it doesn’t: a double booking, an unreadable name, a missed walk-in. More salons are moving every booking into one digital calendar that the whole team can see, with walk-ins recorded the same way as appointments.

[ZainaApp](/zainaapp) gives salons an appointments calendar with walk-ins and real-time booking alerts.

## 2. Clients booking themselves

Clients increasingly prefer to book at their own convenience, often late in the evening. A salon’s own online booking page lets them choose the service and the staff member without calling. ZainaApp includes an online booking page for every salon, where clients can also rate staff.

## 3. WhatsApp as the main channel

In the UAE, WhatsApp is where clients read messages. Salons are moving booking confirmations to WhatsApp rather than relying on calls. ZainaApp sends booking notifications automatically on WhatsApp.

## 4. Knowing every client

Good salons remember their clients: preferred stylist, usual services, when they last came in. Client profiles with full visit history make this possible even as the team changes.

## 5. Fair, transparent commissions

Commissions are one of the most sensitive topics in any salon. When they are calculated by hand at month end, mistakes and disputes follow. Salons are moving to systems that record who performed each service and sold each product, and calculate commissions automatically. ZainaApp tracks commissions on services and products, alongside attendance, performance and warnings.

## 6. Packages and gift cards

Packages and gift cards bring clients back and bring in cash up front. They only work if they are tracked properly, so a client’s remaining sessions or balance is always clear. ZainaApp handles services, packages and gift cards in one place.

## 7. Products and stock under control

Salons use and sell products every day. Without tracking, stock disappears, runs out or expires. Inventory and purchases for salon products belong in the same system as services.

## 8. Real numbers at the end of the day

Owners want to know, quickly: how much did we take today, who performed best, and what do we owe in commissions? End-of-day reports, sales reports and employee performance reports turn those questions into a quick check rather than a long evening.
