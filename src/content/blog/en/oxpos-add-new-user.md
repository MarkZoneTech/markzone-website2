Before you hand the cashier to anyone, give them their own account with the right permissions. This three-minute lesson shows how to create a new user in [OxPOS](/oxpos), with a temporary password they change on their first login.

## Step by step

1. **Open user management.** You’ll see a summary of your current users.
2. **Create the user.** Enter their name and email.
3. **Set a temporary password** and require them to change it at first login.
4. **Choose the role.** Pick a role such as cashier, or set custom permissions that decide exactly which screens they can see.
5. **Enter salary, commission and discount limit.** The monthly salary, their commission rate and the maximum discount they can give.
6. **Save.** A confirmation message appears.
7. **Log in as the new user** with the company code, email and password to check it works.
8. **Confirm the user is in the list.**

## Key points

- Every user logs in with the **company code + email + password**.
- The **discount limit** stops cashiers giving discounts without permission.
- **Commission** is calculated automatically on each employee’s sales.
- **Custom roles** decide exactly which screens a user can see.

## Security tip

Never share one account between several employees. Every sale should be credited to the person who made it, so your reports and commissions are accurate.
