Your product list is the heart of your cashier system. If it is built properly, sales are fast, stock is accurate and reports make sense. This ten-minute lesson shows how to build that list in [OxPOS](/oxpos), whether you have fifty products or several thousand.

## What this video covers

- Adding a new product with its full details: name, SKU, price, category, brand and shelf
- The difference between a **simple product** and a **product with variants** (sizes and colours)
- Adding a barcode and linking it to the product
- **Bulk import** to upload hundreds of products at once
- Linking products to categories, brands and shelves
- Product status (Active or Warning)

## Step by step

1. **Open Products and choose Add Product.** Enter the name, SKU and selling price.
2. **Organise it.** Choose the category, brand and shelf. This makes searching, reporting and stock counts much easier.
3. **Pick the product type.** Use a simple product for single items. Use variants for items sold in sizes or colours, so each variant has its own stock.
4. **Add the barcode.** Scan the existing barcode or enter it manually, so the cashier can scan it at checkout.
5. **Save and check the status.** Active products are ready to sell. A Warning status tells you something needs attention.
6. **Use bulk import for large catalogues.** Prepare your list in a spreadsheet and import it in one step.

## Tips for a clean catalogue

- **Agree on naming first.** Decide how names, brands and categories are written before importing, so reports stay tidy.
- **Use variants for fashion.** A shirt in five sizes is one product with five variants, not five separate products.
- **Set shelves if you have a warehouse area.** Staff find stock faster, and counts are quicker.
- **Weighed products** such as fruit or nuts can be set up to work with a weighing scale.
