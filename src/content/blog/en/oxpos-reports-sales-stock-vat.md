How do you know your shop is really doing well? The answer is in your reports. This six-minute lesson walks through the reports in [OxPOS](/oxpos), which bring every number in your business into one place.

## What this video covers

- The **Sales Summary** report
- **Stock** reports and stock status
- **Financial** reports
- **Tax (VAT)** reports
- **Operations** reports
- **Customer and supplier** reports
- Exporting reports as **PDF**

## Step by step

1. **Open Reports** from the main menu.
2. **Start with the sales summary.** Choose the period and review sales by item, employee and receipt.
3. **Check stock reports** for stock levels and total stock value.
4. **Review financial reports,** including profit and loss, which combine sales, purchases and expenses.
5. **Open the VAT report** when preparing your VAT figures. For tax questions, check with the Federal Tax Authority or your accountant.
6. **Look at customer and supplier reports** for balances and history.
7. **Export as PDF or Excel** to save or share.

## A simple reporting routine

- **Daily:** the end-of-day report and cash difference from the shift.
- **Weekly:** best-selling items and low-stock items.
- **Monthly:** profit and loss, VAT report and supplier balances.

## Why it matters

Reports turn daily activity into decisions: what to reorder, which staff member sells most, and whether a branch is profitable. Because OxPOS keeps sales, stock, purchases, expenses and staff in one system, the reports agree with each other.
