Some of the best introductions to MarkZone come from people who already know us: clients, friends and professionals who work with business owners every day. The MarkZone Growth Partner program is our way of working with those people properly.

## Who can become a Growth Partner?

Anyone who knows business owners who need to get organized, for example:

- **Shop owners** who can introduce neighbouring shops
- **Salon professionals** who know other salons, barbershops and spas
- **Tailors** who know other tailoring shops
- **Advisors and professionals** who work with SMEs every day

## Who can you refer?

Any business that could benefit from what MarkZone offers:

- [OxPOS](/oxpos) for retail shops: supermarkets, fashion and garment stores, cosmetics shops and consignment stores
- [ZainaApp](/zainaapp) for ladies’ salons, barbershops, spas and beauty lounges
- [TexPOS](/texpos) for custom tailoring, ready-made items and alterations
- [Websites](/websites), delivered within 2 weeks
- [Custom software and mobile apps](/custom-software) for businesses with unique processes

## How it works

1. **Join**: message us on WhatsApp to become a Growth Partner. We will explain the program and share the current partner terms.
2. **Refer**: introduce a business owner who needs to get organized.
3. **We take care of them**: we handle the conversation, the demo, setup and support.

## Why partners trust us with their contacts

Referring someone puts your reputation on the line. We take that seriously. Every business you introduce gets the same standard of service as every MarkZone client:

- Onboarding and setup, usually the same day for our apps
- Data migration from Excel, where available
- Full WhatsApp support daily from 10 AM to 10 PM (Sunday off)
- On-site visits when the situation requires
- Honest, clear pricing with no hidden costs
