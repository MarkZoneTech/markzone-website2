A salon is two businesses at once. One is a service business, where time is what you sell. The other is a people business, where clients come back because of a stylist they trust. When appointments are in a notebook, client notes are in people's heads and commissions are calculated at month-end, both businesses suffer.

Here is how UAE salon owners can run things more smoothly.

## 1. Keep one calendar for everyone

When the phone, Instagram messages and walk-ins all create bookings in different places, double-bookings are only a matter of time. Use one calendar for the whole salon, including walk-ins, so every stylist and the front desk see the same day. ZainaApp gives you an appointments calendar with real-time booking alerts, so the team knows the moment something changes.

## 2. Let clients book online and confirm on WhatsApp

Many clients prefer to book at night, when your phone is off. An online booking page lets them choose the service and the staff member and book on their own. ZainaApp gives each salon its own booking page, where clients can also rate staff. Booking notifications go out automatically on WhatsApp, which is where your clients already are.

## 3. Sell packages and gift cards

A package turns one visit into several, and a gift card brings you a new client through someone who already trusts you. Set up your services, packages and gift cards once, and your team can sell them at checkout instead of explaining them from memory each time.

## 4. Know your clients by name

A client who is remembered comes back. Keep a profile for each one with her full visit history, so any member of staff can see what she had last time and what she usually books. It is a small thing that makes clients feel looked after, and it does not depend on one stylist being on shift.

## 5. Manage staff with facts, not feelings

Attendance, performance, commissions and warnings are the parts of salon life that cause the most friction when they are unclear. Record them as they happen. ZainaApp tracks staff attendance, performance, commissions on services and products, and warnings. When everyone can see how the numbers are built, there is less to argue about. Our guide to [fair staff commissions](/blog/staff-commission-tracking-compliance) covers this in detail.

## 6. Treat salon products like stock

The shampoos, colours and retail products you sell are money on a shelf. Track purchases and inventory for your salon products so you know what you have and what to reorder, before a client asks for something you ran out of.

## 7. Close the day properly

Check out clients through a point of sale with sequential invoice numbers, then look at the end-of-day report. Over time, the sales, employee performance and commission reports show which days are busy, who your strongest performers are, and where the salon really earns.
