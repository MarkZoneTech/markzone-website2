Your team is your most valuable asset, and keeping their details, pay and access organised protects both them and your business. This lesson shows the employee tools in [OxPOS](/oxpos), where salaries and expenses sit in the same system as your sales.

## What this video covers

- The **Employee Management** dashboard and its indicators
- **Adding a new employee**
- Setting **salary and commission** for each employee
- The **maximum discount** each person can give
- **App access** permissions
- Managing **advances**
- The **salary payments** record
- Activating or deactivating an employee

## Step by step

1. **Open Employee Management** and review the summary.
2. **Add an employee.** Enter their details and monthly salary.
3. **Set commission.** If they earn commission on sales, enter the rate.
4. **Set the maximum discount.** This protects your margins at the counter.
5. **Decide on app access.** Give access only to staff who need to log in, such as cashiers.
6. **Record advances** when an employee takes money ahead of payday.
7. **Pay salaries.** Record each payment so the history is clear.
8. **Deactivate leavers** instead of deleting them, so their history stays in your reports.

## Tips

- **Match access to the job.** Not every employee needs to log in. The next lessons explain the difference between employees and users.
- **Review commission monthly** with the employee, using the report.
- **Deactivate access immediately** when someone leaves.
