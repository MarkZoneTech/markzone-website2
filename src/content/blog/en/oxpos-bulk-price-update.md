When a supplier raises costs or you plan a seasonal offer, updating prices one by one can take a whole day. The bulk price tool in [OxPOS](/oxpos) changes a full category, a brand or even your entire stock in one step, with a preview before anything is saved.

## Three ways to change prices

1. **Direct change:** increase or decrease prices by a percentage or a fixed amount.
2. **Selling price = cost + margin:** OxPOS calculates the price from each product’s cost.
3. **Cost = selling price − margin:** useful when you know the shelf price and want to set the cost side.

## Step by step

1. **Open Products and press the bulk edit button.**
2. **Choose the mode:** direct change, cost plus margin, or price minus margin.
3. **Choose the scope:** all products, one category, one brand, or products you select by hand.
4. **Enter the value:** a percentage or a fixed amount, and choose the rounding rule so prices end neatly.
5. **Include variants** if the change should apply to sizes and colours too.
6. **Press Preview.** Check the old and new prices side by side.
7. **Apply.** The new prices are live on the POS straight away.

## When to use it

- Raising prices after a supplier increases costs
- Preparing a seasonal offer on one category
- Setting the same profit margin across a whole category

## Tips

- **Always preview first.** The tool can change hundreds of products at once.
- **Use rounding.** Prices such as 19.50 or 20.00 are easier for customers and cashiers than 19.37.
- **Record why you changed prices,** for example the supplier name and date, so the team understands the new shelf prices.
