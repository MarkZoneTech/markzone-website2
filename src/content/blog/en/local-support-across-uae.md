Buying software is the easy part. What really matters is what happens next: getting set up, training your team, and knowing someone will answer when you need help. Here is exactly how support works at MarkZone, for every client, whether you choose a ready-made app or a custom system.

## 1. Onboarding and setup

We start by setting up the system for your business: your products or services, your staff, your branches and your preferences. Setup for our apps usually takes between 45 minutes and 2 hours, and most businesses are running the same day.

Your team is trained as part of onboarding, so staff are comfortable before the first busy day.

## 2. Bringing your data with you

Retyping hundreds of items or customers is one of the biggest reasons businesses delay switching systems. Where your data is available in Excel, we migrate it for you as part of onboarding.

## 3. WhatsApp support, every day except Sunday

Once you are running, support is on WhatsApp, the channel UAE businesses already use all day. Full WhatsApp support is available daily from 10 AM to 10 PM, with Sunday off. You reach real people in the UAE who know the products.

## 4. On-site visits when needed

Some things are faster to fix in person. When the situation requires, we visit. MarkZone is based in Dubai and serves businesses in all 7 emirates.

## 5. The same standard for everyone

Outstanding service is the standard for every customer. It is never a paid upgrade, and it does not depend on the size of your business. A single shop gets the same care as a multi-branch group.

## What this looks like in practice

A typical start with MarkZone:

1. **You message us on WhatsApp** and tell us what you sell and how you work.
2. **We recommend the right app**, or a custom solution if nothing ready-made fits.
3. **We set up the system**, migrate your data from Excel where available, and train your team.
4. **You go live**, usually the same day for our apps.
5. **You keep us on WhatsApp** for questions, changes and help.

## Why we work this way

MarkZone started because we saw UAE businesses paying excessive prices for software and getting poor support afterwards. Local support, honest pricing and speed are core values for us, not marketing lines.
