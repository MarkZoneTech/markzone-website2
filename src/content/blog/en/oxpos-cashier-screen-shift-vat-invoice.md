This is the practical cashier lesson for [OxPOS](/oxpos): from opening the shift to completing the sale and printing a tax invoice. It is made for cashiers with no previous experience, and for owners who want to train new staff quickly.

## Step by step

1. **Open the shift.** Before the first sale, open a shift and enter the opening cash in the drawer. Every sale is then linked to the active shift.
2. **Find the product.** Scan the barcode or search by name. Items go straight into the basket.
3. **Browse by category** when an item has no barcode. Tap the category, then the product.
4. **Use the quick tools.** Switch to grid view, park a sale to serve someone else, process a return, or open the sales history.
5. **Take payment.** Choose the payment method. Quick cash buttons (50, 100, 200, 500) speed up change, and one invoice can combine several payment methods.
6. **Complete the sale and print.** The receipt prints as a tax invoice with 5% VAT and a QR code.
7. **Close the shift at the end of the day.** OxPOS shows expected versus actual cash and gives you an end-of-day report.

## What you will learn

- Opening and closing a shift, and linking every sale to it
- Barcode scanning and quick product search
- Parking an invoice and returning to it later
- Combining payment methods in one invoice
- Quick cash denomination buttons
- A 5% VAT tax invoice with a QR code

## Tips for smoother checkouts

- **Count the drawer honestly at opening.** An accurate opening balance makes the end-of-day report meaningful.
- **Park instead of cancelling.** Parked sales keep the basket safe while the customer finds one more item.
- **Give each cashier their own login,** so every sale is credited to the right person.
