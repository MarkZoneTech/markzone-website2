A brand should say something true about a company. When we looked at what MarkZone does every day, one idea kept coming back: helping businesses get organized, so things get done. The logo and colours grew from that idea.

## A check mark in motion

At the centre of the MarkZone logo is a check mark. It is the simplest symbol of "done": the order prepared, the stock counted, the day closed correctly. That is what we want our apps to give business owners, a feeling that things are under control.

Around it, the swooshes suggest movement. Business never stands still, and neither do the shops, salons and tailors we work with. The check mark sits inside that movement: organization that keeps up with a busy day.

## Navy: trust and stability

Navy is the foundation of the brand. Businesses trust us with their sales, stock and staff records, and that trust has to be earned and kept. Navy stands for reliability and professionalism, the quiet confidence that the system will work tomorrow as it did today.

## Teal: technology that feels approachable

Teal carries the word "Technology" in our logo. It represents the digital side of what we do, but in a fresh, approachable way. Our apps should feel simple enough for any staff member, not like complicated corporate software.

## Orange: action

Orange is the check mark itself. It is the colour of action: getting set up, getting the answer, getting it done. We use it carefully, for the moments that matter most, like the button that starts a conversation with us.

## A family of brands

MarkZone is the parent brand, but each app has its own identity made for its industry:

- [OxPOS](/oxpos), with its ox-head emblem, made for retail
- [ZainaApp](/zainaapp), with its gold "Z", made for salons and spas
- [TexPOS](/texpos), with its gold scissors, made for bespoke tailoring

Each one speaks to its own customers, while sharing the same values: local support, honest pricing, speed, and software built around your workflow.

## What stays the same

Colours and logos can evolve. What they represent should not: helping UAE businesses get organized and grow their sales. That is the promise behind our tagline, **Organize your business. Grow your sales.**
