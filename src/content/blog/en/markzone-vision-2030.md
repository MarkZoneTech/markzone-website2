Every company needs to know where it is going. At MarkZone, our direction comes from the businesses we work with every day: shops, salons, tailors and growing SMEs across the UAE.

## Where we started

MarkZone started from a passion for programming and electronics. We saw UAE businesses paying excessive prices for software and getting poor support afterwards, so we set out to offer solutions SMEs can afford, built around how they actually work.

We began with a free subscription app. As customers asked for more, we grew into full business solutions, starting with accounting. Along the way, we learned something important: many SMEs were not failing because of weak marketing or accounting. They were failing because they were not organized internally, and that was costing them sales and leads. Organizing the business became the core of what our apps do.

## Our mission

**To help UAE SMEs solve their daily problems with apps that make every working day easier, more organized, and more productive.**

That mission shapes every product decision. Each app is made for a specific industry rather than being a generic system:

- [OxPOS](/oxpos) for retail shops
- [ZainaApp](/zainaapp) for salons, barbershops and spas
- [TexPOS](/texpos) for tailoring shops

Next, MadaPOS for restaurants and cafés and AqarOS for real estate agencies are [coming soon](/coming-soon).

## Our vision

**To bring the solutions we built for SMEs to large-scale businesses, expanding in stages into high-end enterprise solutions.**

We believe the lessons that make a small shop organized, such as clear stock, fair staff management and honest reporting, apply at every size. Enterprise and governance solutions are on our roadmap, and we will roll them out in stages rather than all at once. Our priority remains the SMEs who rely on us today.

## The values behind it

1. **Local support**: real people in the UAE who answer.
2. **Honest pricing**: fair and clear, with no hidden costs.
3. **Speed**: quick setup, quick fixes, quick delivery.
4. **Built around your workflow**: software that fits how you work.

## Where we are today

More than 400 businesses have trusted MarkZone since 2019. More than 15 businesses joined our new-generation apps within their first months. We serve all 7 emirates from Dubai, and our apps are typically running the same day.

We are proud of that progress, and we know the next stage is earned one well-supported customer at a time.
