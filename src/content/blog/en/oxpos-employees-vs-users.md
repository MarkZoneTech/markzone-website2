A question we hear often: why does [OxPOS](/oxpos) have an “Employees” section and a “Users” section, and do you need to add someone to both? This lesson answers it in two and a half minutes.

## The short answer

- **Users** are the people who **log in** to the system.
- **Employees** are the people who **receive a salary**.

A person can be in one section or both, depending on their job.

## The rule in practice

| Role | Section | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse worker or driver | Employee only | Salary, no login |
| Cashier | Employee + user | Salary and login access |
| External accountant | User only | Login, no salary |

## Step by step

1. **Add the employee.** Enter their details and salary.
2. **Decide on login access.** Tick “grant login access” only if they need to use the system.
3. **Review the employees dashboard** for salaries, advances and the average salary.
4. **Record advances** when needed.
5. **Pay the salary.** Commission is calculated automatically where it applies.
6. **Follow up advances and amounts due** from the same screen.

## Why it matters

Separating pay from access keeps your system secure. Only the people who really need to log in can see your data, while everyone on the payroll is still recorded properly.
