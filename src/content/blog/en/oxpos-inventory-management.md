Disorganised stock costs money: items run out without warning, extra stock ties up cash, and nobody knows the real value on the shelves. This four-minute lesson shows how [OxPOS](/oxpos) gives you an accurate picture of your stock at any moment.

## What this video covers

- The **Inventory Management** dashboard and how to read its figures
- The current stock level for every product
- **Low Stock** and **Out of Stock** alerts
- **Adjust Stock** for corrections after a count or damage
- **Transfer Stock** between branches
- Bulk import for stock
- The total value of your stock in AED

## Step by step

1. **Open Inventory Management.** The summary shows how many products you have and which need attention.
2. **Review the alerts.** Low Stock and Out of Stock lists are your reorder list.
3. **Adjust stock when needed.** After a stock count, a damaged item or a correction, adjust the quantity so the system matches the shelf.
4. **Transfer between branches.** If one branch has extra stock, transfer it to a branch that needs it.
5. **Use bulk import** to load opening quantities for many products at once.
6. **Check the stock value.** Total stock value in AED helps you understand how much cash is sitting on the shelves.

## Tips

- **Count one category a week.** Small, regular counts are easier than one big annual count.
- **Always adjust, never ignore.** If the shelf and the system disagree, correct it straight away.
- **Link purchases to stock.** When you record purchase invoices in OxPOS, stock updates automatically.
